About

I am a PhD student in Population Health Science (Epidemiology track) at Harvard University, advised by James Robins. My research interests include epidemiologic methods, causal inference, missing data methods, and their applications to chronic disease prevention and public health.

I received my M.S. in Epidemiology from Harvard University (2023), advised by Molin Wang, and dual B.S. degrees in Medical Science and Economics from Peking University (2021). I have published papers in both biostatistics and epidemiology, with a focus on methodological innovations and public health applications.
